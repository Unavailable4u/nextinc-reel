# Glossary

**Plate** - the photograph, used as the background. **Match camera** - the virtual camera pose and lens that make a virtual desk plane line up with the photo's desk. **Plate-follow** - moving the plate with the camera so the books stay registered on the desk. **M (magnification)** - on-screen plate width divided by 736 px. **Regime R1-R4** - the four camera situations (guide 05). **Operator model** - noise layers that make the camera behave like a person. **Pile / riser** - the three-book stack at the left; the two-tier display at the right. **Spread** - two facing pages. **Riffle** - fanning the page block with a thumb. **Fore-edge** - the side of the page block opposite the spine. **Gutter** - where the two pages meet at the spine. **Foil** - metallic stamping on a cover. **Bookcloth** - the woven fabric covering the boards. **Boards / squares** - the stiff cover panels / the part that overhangs the pages. **Colophon** - the page of production facts. **Contact shadow** - the tight dark line where an object touches a surface. **DOF** - depth of field: the blur of things out of focus. **CoC** - circle of confusion: the blur radius of an out-of-focus point. **Bokeh** - the look of the blur. **IBL / probe** - lighting and reflection from an environment image. **SSIM** - a similarity score between two images (1.0 is identical). **Beat / bar** - 30 / 120 frames at 60 fps and 120 BPM. **Truth rule** - facts on screen come from the site snapshot. **Gate** - a pass/fail checkpoint (G0-G4). **Snapshot** - `src/data/site-snapshot.json`.
