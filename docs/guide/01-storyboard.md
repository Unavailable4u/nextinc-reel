# 01 - Storyboard: the whole film

**Working title:** *The Reading Desk.* Site title (decision for later): "Next Inc. - what we have built so far" (the wording the site's ledger already suggests).
**Spec:** 1920x1080, 60 fps, **5400 frames = 90.000 s**, 120 BPM (1 beat = 30 f, 1 bar = 120 f, 45 bars). Every cut, lift, page landing and label lands on a beat. Works muted (nothing essential is only in the audio).

**Idea in one line:** *a lab's work, kept as books on a desk, read by someone who cares.* The film is entirely in-world. There are no captions floating over the picture, except the thin figure-style labels in S23 and the logo at the end.

## Camera regimes (used in the table)

| Regime | Name | What the camera does | What the photo does |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Establishing | Nearly still, tiny drift | Shown 1:1, pillarboxed into its own dark edges |
| R2 | Plate-follow | Walks/dollies toward or away from the desk, small pan | Scales and shifts with the camera, with a growing defocus |
| R3 | Hero | The book is held in front of the camera at reading distance | Only a defocused glow behind it (amber strips, dark wall) |
| R4 | Truck / display | Slides right at desk height, then settles | Pans (zoomed in), defocused except on the display |

Full rules and numbers: `05-camera-and-motion.md`.

## Act by act

### Act 0 - The plate (S00, 0:00-0:04)
- **Image:** your photograph, exactly as supplied, centred with its own dark left and right edges fading into black (the left edge is nearly black already, the right edge is dark brown; a soft feather of about 60 photo pixels hides the seam). 2.5% slow push-in. A few **dust motes** drift in the lamp light (3D particles, seeded). Fine film grain.
- **Do not:** animate, relight or retouch anything inside the photo.
- **The pile is already there**, left of the keyboard, between the lamp and the keyboard mat: three small hardcover books, a few pixels tall at this size. Most viewers will not notice them yet. That is the point; the camera will find them.
- **Sound:** room tone fades in over 1 s (very low), a faint lamp hum, a single distant clock-like tick on bar 2 is optional.
- **Cut rule:** S00 hands over to S01 without a cut: the push-in simply continues and accelerates on beat 8 (frame 240).

### Act 1 - Someone goes to the table (S01-S03, 0:04-0:16)
- **S01 (240-479):** the camera is a person walking in. It moves in a gentle arc toward the lamp side of the desk with a soft footstep bob (see the operator model in guide 05). The photo scales up; defocus begins at the far wall.
- **S02 (480-839):** the camera slows ("they have arrived"), leans in, and the focus pull moves from the far wall to the pile. The three books resolve: slightly askew, the top one a little off-centre. The lamp is the key light on their left; the amber under-shelf strip is a rim light from behind. **Spines face the camera** and read, top to bottom: `MiniMe`, `MiniLocker`, `SPHEREx`. Contact shadows are soft and warm.
- **S03 (840-959):** hold on the stack for one bar. Not still: a slow lateral drift of 1.5 cm and a 3% push (rule: no shot is still). A page-edge catches the lamp light.
- **Sound:** footsteps are not heard (room is carpeted); only cloth rustle of the operator, then a quiet breath-like pause at the arrival.

### Act 2 - The first book (S04-S05, 0:16-0:24)
- **S04 (960-1079):** an anticipation beat (the stack compresses 1 mm as the top book is gripped), then the book slides out toward the camera, lifts, and rotates up to face us. It has **weight**: slight overshoot, a settle, a sway. The pile's other two books stay on the desk, the shadow of the lifted book moves across them.
- **S05 (1080-1439):** the cover in full view at reading distance. Background: pure defocus, amber bokeh bars where the shelf LEDs are, dark wall. Slow orbit of +-8 degrees yaw and a specular sweep across the foil (title and logo) timed to cross the logo on beat 3 of bar 10 (frame 1260). Hold at least 3 s with the full cover readable. Content of the cover: guide 04.
- **Sound:** soft thud of the cover board lifting off the stack, cloth on cloth, a held breath.

### Act 3 - Opening the book (S06-S09, 0:24-0:32)
- **S06 (1440-1559):** the front board opens about 165 degrees on its spine hinge with a damped overshoot; the inside shows the endpaper (pattern generated from the project's palette and motif) on the left board and the half-title page on the right.
- **S07 (1560-1679):** first page turn: the half-title lifts at the fore-edge corner, bends, passes vertical (it is lit from behind by the lamp: a warm translucent glow), and lands. Spread: left is the half-title's back (blank), right is the **title page**: `MiniMe`, subtitle from the data, the three author names, `Next Inc.` and the year.
- **S08 (1680-1799):** second turn. Spread: left page **Abstract.** (a short paragraph from the data, with the small-caps lead-in the site uses); right page **Fig. 1** (the project's cover image or hero screenshot) with a caption.
- **S09 (1800-1919):** hold. The camera drifts down the right page to the caption.
- **Sound:** one distinct page-turn per turn; fabric creak on the cover.

### Act 4 - The middle of the book (S10-S13, 0:32-0:46)
- **S10 (1920-2039):** the riffle. The reader's thumb fans the page block: 10-14 sheets whip across over 1.5 s, accelerating then decelerating, the page block visibly thick. Slight motion blur (guide 03). Lands on the middle of the book.
- **S11 (2040-2279):** **Gallery spread.** Two or three real screenshots "attached" like photographs with white borders and tiny corner mounts, each with a caption (at most 8 words) saying what you are looking at. The camera pushes slowly toward one screenshot, then another.
- **S12 (2280-2519):** **Film spread.** The promo film's thumbnail in a frame with a play mark, the duration (`1:03`), a one-line caption, and a small "Interactive edition" tag: an e-book that *could* play it. It does **not** play. A page turn from S11 to S12 uses the full turn animation.
- **S13 (2520-2759):** **Links spread.** Two QR codes on the right page (the project's case study and the film), with the web addresses set in monospace beside them; on the left a short "Read on the web" column. The camera holds square-on for at least 2 s so the QR codes are scannable (rule in guide 07).
- **Sound:** riffle flutter (the loudest paper sound in the film), a soft settle.

### Act 5 - Put it down (S14-S15, 0:46-0:50)
- **S14 (2760-2879):** the pages flip back to the front, the board closes with a soft clap (a quiet air puff), and the back cover glimpses past.
- **S15 (2880-2999):** the book is set down on the desk **beside** the pile (not back on it), contact shadow tightening as it lowers, a low thud and a 4-frame settle. The camera returns from R3 to R2 and eases to the pile, whose top book is now MiniLocker.

### Act 6 - The second book (S16-S21, 0:50-1:06)
Same grammar, shorter: the viewer has learned the language, so every step is about 30% faster.
- **S16:** lift, with no pile hold before it: the pile is already close from S15, so the book goes straight up (2 s instead of the 4 s that S03-S04 took).
- **S17:** cover hero, 4 s: parchment-coloured cloth, the concentric-ring mark, `MiniLocker` with `Locker` in crimson.
- **S18:** open, straight to the title page (one turn).
- **S19:** one short riffle, then the gallery spread with the Attack Lab and Blast Radius screenshots.
- **S20:** the film spread (the 2:50 film) and the QR codes.
- **S21:** close and set down next to the first book. Two books now lie beside a pile of one.

### Act 7 - To the right (S22-S23, 1:06-1:20)
- **S22 (3960-4319):** the camera pulls back from the pile and slides right along the desk. The plate pans. The keyboard drifts by (defocused), then the **chair back fills the frame** for about 1 s of dark, soft leather. **Under this wipe the set is swapped** (continuity ledger below). The chair slides out of frame and the right end of the desk arrives.
- **S23 (4320-4799):** the **riser**: a small two-tier walnut display with four books standing, covers to camera, in site order: `I Mini Me`, `II SPHEREx`, `III MiniLocker`, `IV Immersive`. Labels in the site's figure style (hairline leader, small CMU Serif italic) appear one per beat starting at frame 4380, each saying the project name, year and status as the data states them. A soft highlight sweep passes over each book as its label arrives.

### Act 8 - The mark (S24-S25, 1:20-1:30)
- **S24 (4800-5279):** over 2 s the room dims (exposure -1.5 stops, the lamp warms and falls), the books stay softly lit by the LED strips. The **Next Inc.** wordmark draws in (the crimson italic **x** is stroked on, then the letters rise into place), then the tagline "Where research becomes working software." and the address `wearenext.tech`, all read from the data.
- **S25 (5280-5399):** hold 1.5 s, 0.5 s fade to black. Room tone fades over the last 1 s.

## Overlays (the only 2D elements)

1. Labels in S23: hairline leader (0.75 px at 1080p), a 5 px dot, text in CMU Serif Italic 26 px (title) with a mono sub-line 18 px (`2026 - completed`). Colour: ink on a faint paper chip, `#F5F0E6` at 88%.
2. The wordmark in S24: vector path of the site's glyph (`nextinc/scripts/brand/glyph-x.json` has the **x** path), CMU Serif Bold Italic for the rest.
3. Grain and vignette (global).
